How it works

Request. Review. Confirm.

A booking is a request, not an instant dispatch. That is deliberate: the driver checks availability and the route before anything is agreed.

01
You submit a request

Trip type, pickup, destination, date, time, passengers and luggage. The estimate appears before you send it.

02
The driver reviews it

He may call you about the route, timing or an unusual pickup. First-time bookings are confirmed by phone.

03
You get a confirmation

With a booking reference, the agreed fare and the pickup window. Both of you work from the same record.

04
The trip runs as booked

Extra stops or additional passengers are agreed and re-priced, never added quietly mid-trip.

Safety

Procedures, not promises

OnTyme reduces risk. It cannot eliminate it, and it does not claim to.

Verified first bookings

New customers give a name and phone number, confirmed by a short call before the first trip.

Recorded trips

Route, time and reference are stored against the booking for both sides.

No unplanned detours

Changes to the route or passenger count are agreed before they happen.

The driver may decline

If a booking feels unsafe, he can refuse or cancel it. That authority is part of the service.
